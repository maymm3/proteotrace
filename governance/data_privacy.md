# Data privacy and research governance

ProteoTrace is a research portfolio demonstration, not clinical software.

- Use synthetic or openly licensed data for public demonstrations.
- Do not upload direct identifiers, re-identifiable combinations of variables, raw clinical records, or unpublished partner data.
- Maintain institutional ethics approval, data-use agreements, and access controls before using real clinical datasets.
- A language model may describe validated tool outputs but must not independently calculate, diagnose, recommend treatment, or override expert review.
- Every reported scientific claim should link to a deterministic result file or a cited source.
