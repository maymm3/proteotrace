# Google research application readiness plan

## Current status

ProteoTrace now provides a public, reproducible research prototype with synthetic data, deterministic QC and statistical analysis, automated tests, governance documents, and a local interface.

## What cannot be completed by software alone

The major Google research awards are normally led by eligible academic faculty or Principal Investigators. A future direct student route is the Google PhD Fellowship, which requires full-time PhD enrollment and nomination by an eligible university. This repository is evidence for applications, not a substitute for institutional eligibility.

## Evidence to build next

1. Recruit a PhD supervisor or academic collaborator in computational biology, biomedical AI, or clinical proteomics.
2. Co-design a bounded research question using approved public data.
3. Add benchmark experiments measuring numerical accuracy, reproducibility, reliability, cost, and human-review burden.
4. Publish a preprint, poster, workshop abstract, or open benchmark with the collaborating institution.
5. Apply for research credits and, when eligible, a PhD Fellowship or faculty-led research award.

## Proposal positioning

**Working title:** Auditable agentic workflows for robust biomarker discovery from longitudinal proteomics data.

**Problem:** Biomedical teams need efficient analysis support, but opaque AI-generated claims can undermine reproducibility and safe interpretation.

**Contribution:** A constrained agent framework that calls deterministic QC and statistical tools, links every written finding to machine-generated results or literature sources, and evaluates reliability using controlled synthetic and public-data benchmarks.

**Impact:** Reusable, transparent infrastructure for researchers working with complex protein-biomarker datasets.
