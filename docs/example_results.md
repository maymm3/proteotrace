# Synthetic demonstration results

This example uses only the repository's synthetic dataset. It is not evidence about real patients, diseases, biomarkers, or treatments.

## What the deterministic workflow recovered

The synthetic ground truth intentionally makes IL6 and CRP higher in the synthetic responder group. The transparent Welch t-test ranks both proteins first, demonstrating that the pipeline recovers the intended signal.

| Rank | Protein | Mean difference, responder minus non-responder | Interpretation |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 1 | IL6 | 6.45 | Expected synthetic signal recovered |
| 2 | CRP | 7.55 | Expected synthetic signal recovered |
| 3 | VEGFA | 0.70 | Small synthetic difference, requires interpretation |

## What a researcher must still check

- Study design, sample provenance, preprocessing, and suitability of the statistical model.
- Multiple-testing correction, covariates, longitudinal structure, and biological plausibility for a real study.
- Any claim generated by a future language-model layer against the exported CSV results and original literature source.
